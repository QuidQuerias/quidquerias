Video / Case study

Making Renewable Energy Personal

Hitachi (Energy Harbor)

Client
Hitachi
Agency
Matter Communications
Role
Led edit and script/narrative development
Account
$75K of Hitachi post-production in a single quarter, across 4 final videos

We worked with Hitachi to tell the story of their collaboration with Energy Harbor in Ohio supporting renewable energy development across the region. Angelita of EH and her daughter participated (even letting the crew shoot around their home), and Uday brought the audience along for a hike.

I received the footage via hard drive and catalogued all the material, and led the rest of the edit all the way through revisions and even some extra 3rd party sound design.
