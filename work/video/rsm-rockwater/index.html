An RSM Customer Success Story

Client
RSM
Agency
Matter Communications
Role
Led edit and script/narrative development pairing tour with interview
Format
About 11 minutes, with a 3-person interview and a full facilities tour
Account
$100K of RSM video work per year

One of the many types of projects we'd work on with staple accounting consulting client, RSM, was customer success stories where we'd unpack the hurdles of small and mid-sized businesses, and how RSM's suite of services helped them overcome their challenges.

Usually these would clock in around the 3 to 4 minute mark, but this one was an outlier in a few ways. Since it had to contain a 3-person interview and exhaustive facilities tour, it's almost 11 minutes long.

This one was all about finding the right balance of people to industry while still showcasing as much of the site as possible.
