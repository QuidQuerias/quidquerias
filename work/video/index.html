Video

When you're the "fix it in post" guy long enough you really begin to crave some input in other parts of the process. It's hardly a wonder that the producer and editor roles have become so entwined (read: conflated) in recent years as the industry adapts to spurts of automation.

Around June 2026, the editing contract work I was doing for an Independent Digital Creator (100k+ subscribers) dried up abruptly and I've been applying to new full-time opportunities since. It's bleak out there, y'all!

Prior to that, I worked as a Contract Video Editor for Pennant Video which focuses on the marketing trifecta of anthem, explainer, and endorsement videos for organizations spanning manufacturing, healthcare, education, business, and technology. The work there was a focused/pared down version of what I did before that as a full-time, 3-year Video Editor at Matter Communications (the unit rebranded as "Gusto"). I worked closely with individual clients and full teams to identify internal and external marketing and communications needs and execute media projects with post-production budgets ranging from $25,000-80,000 5-7x per quarter.

I've continued to hone fluent capabilities in Adobe Premiere Pro, Photoshop, and After Effects to carry out all post-production needs from ingest/transcription (logging large bodies of footage 1+ TB in 4K using proxy workflows), through script drafting, editing, mastering, color correction, keying, digital screen compositing, data visualization, lower thirds, titles, logo/still-asset animation, client revision cycles, and final encoding/compression.

Below, you'll find some of my past projects along with some context to shed light on production challenges, workflows, and results.
