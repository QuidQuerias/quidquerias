No Production? No Problem!

Telling Stories Anyway (with Stock)

Client
Hitachi
Agency
Matter Communications
Role
Led edit, sourced and documented all additional stock footage to fill visual gaps in script
Account
$75K of Hitachi post-production in a single quarter, across 4 final videos

This Hitachi project about AI-powered analytics insights is almost 100% Hitachi archival and stock footage combed from over 4 sites, cleared by/purchased for the client, and compiled along with a professional scripted narration and a smattering of footage from past Hitachi projects.

Since there was no "production" component to this project, I was mostly left to my own devices to source the relevant footage in accordance with a finalized script, clear that with the client, and try to illustrate the story.
