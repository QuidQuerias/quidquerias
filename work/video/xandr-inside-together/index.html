Video / Case study

Building Community During A Pandemic

Xandr (Inside Together)

Client
Xandr
Agency
Matter Communications
Role
Led edit, script/narrative development, and mograph team collaboration
Format
4-part series built entirely from employee-submitted footage
Account
$30K+ per quarter for a full year

The first of a four-part project for (then AT&T, now) Microsoft's advertising tech company, Xandr. I made this series, along with support from the ever-amazing mograph team at Matter, entirely from employee-submitted footage. Can you say: "mixed bag?"

This was a few months into the lockdowns in the Eastern US and people were itching to show community any way they could. Thanks to a great cast of characters in the Xandr community, this surprisingly charming series emerged!
