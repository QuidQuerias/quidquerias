The MiCROTEC / Lucidyne Merger

Client
MiCROTEC
Agency
Matter Communications
Role
Led edit and script/narrative development; created an entire second script that became a separate piece from additional material
Budget
$60K, with an on-site shoot

Acquisitions can be an anxious time for employees. "What (or who) are they going to keep?" It's a time for clear communication.

In the case of the acquisition of Oregon-based Lucidyne by their European lumber-scanning counterpart, MiCROTEC, the message was one of continuity and expanded opportunity. It was also a chance to take a deeper dive into the surprisingly high-tech world of lumber refinement.
